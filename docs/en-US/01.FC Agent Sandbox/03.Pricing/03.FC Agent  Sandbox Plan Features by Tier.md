* Notes：<br>
1.**Deep Sleep Wake-up Efficiency under High Concurrency**: Refers to the total time required to restore deep-sleep instances and rebuild network connections in a high-concurrency scenario (10,000+ concurrent requests) with a 2C4G instance specification.<br>
2.**Deep Sleep**: Comparable to E2B's Pause/Resume functionality. The maximum lifetime for an active (unpaused) sandbox is 24 hours for Eco/Std plans and 7 days for the Pro plan. The timer automatically resets upon pausing, granting the full duration again upon resumption. By cycling through "pause-resume-pause-resume," indefinite online availability can be achieved.<br>
3.**Shallow Sleep**: Exclusive to the Pro plan. Leveraging request-awareness and memory snapshot technologies, instances automatically enter shallow sleep when idle (CPU usage < 0.01 core / Network traffic < 1,000 Bytes/s / No incoming requests) to reduce costs (CPU is not billed during shallow sleep). Instances are woken up ultra-fast within 1 ms upon receiving new requests.<br>
> Feature differences across subscription plans will continue to evolve. Stay tuned!

## Subscription Plan Management
**Purchase & Billing**：No activation required; billed on a pay-as-you-go basis. Set the subscription plan when creating or managing a Team, with options for Eco, Std, or Pro tiers.<br>
**Activation Mechanism**: Subscription plan attributes are inherited from the Team level downwards. All Templates and Sandboxes under the current Team automatically synchronize with these plan attributes, ensuring global consistency.<br>
**Changes & Management**：<br>
* Upgrade: Supports one-way seamless upgrades (Eco → Std → Pro), which can be performed directly in the Team settings.<br>
* Downgrade: To prevent service disruption caused by feature reduction for existing workloads, online downgrades are currently not supported. If you need to downgrade, please create a new Team or contact technical support (DingTalk Group: 179855020297).<br>
